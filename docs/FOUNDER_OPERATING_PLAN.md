## Mission (one line)
Be the default **honest stock desk** on Solana — truth, safe routes, credit without selling.

## Now → Stocklana → Colosseum → Beta → Scale

```
Ship complete desk  →  Submit Stocklana  →  Judge feedback
        ↓                      ↓                    ↓
   Socials + /beta     Credibility boost      Closed beta users
        ↓                      ↓                    ↓
   Traction metrics  →  Submit Colosseum / World's Fair
        ↓
   Funded micro-fills → Revenue rails → Retention compound
```

## North-star metrics

| Metric | Definition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Funded active** | Wallets with balance + ≥1 action / 30d | Real users |
| **Honest quotes** | Quotes shown with live/cached/stale labels | Trust |
| **Wash refuse rate** | Blocked dirty/missing tape (honest) | Moat visible |
| **D30 retention** | Funded users returning day 30 | PMF |
| **Revenue mix** | Trade vs credit/earn vs sub | Bear survival |

Ignore vanity: follower spikes without funded actives.

## Budget & policy (until funded)

- Demo budget ≤ ~$1 · **broadcast paused** by default
- No custom mainnet program deploy on this budget
- Never claim live fills/borrows unless actually confirmed
- Rotate any chat-pasted secrets after hackathon windows

---

## Kill criteria (honesty)

Stop or pivot a feature if, after 6 weeks of real users:
- D30 funded < 10% and no qualitative love for honesty wedge
- Only bounty hunters, zero holdings habit
- Compliance blocks geo without a viable non-US wedge
